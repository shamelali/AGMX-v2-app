# AGMX v2.0 — Sprint 4 Spec: Notice + AGM Pack + Communications

Source: AGMX Master PRD v2.0 (GOV-03, GOV-06, GOV-07).

## Objective
Notice lifecycle, AGM Pack versioning, and communications with delivery/audit trail.

## Notice Composer (GOV-03)
- Auto-calculate deadlines
- Source rule, due-soon/overdue status
- Evidence of completion
- Notice delivery lifecycle: draft → scheduled → delivered → acknowledged

## AGM Pack Manager (GOV-07)
- Versioned documents
- Acknowledgement
- Controlled replacement
- Access history

## Communications (GOV-06)
- Generate/send notices, reminders and updates
- Delivery/open/acknowledgement audit
- Communication Log screen (screen 11)

## Acceptance criteria (this sprint)
- [ ] Notice can be composed with delivery lifecycle and due-soon/overdue status.
- [ ] Notice delivery produces evidence of completion.
- [ ] AGM Pack documents are versioned with controlled replacement.
- [ ] AGM Pack acknowledgement is recorded per participant.
- [ ] Communications (notices, reminders, updates) have delivery/open/acknowledgement audit.
- [ ] Access history retained for AGM Pack documents.
