# AGMX v2.0 — Sprint 12 Spec: Minutes + AI

Source: AGMX Master PRD v2.0 (GOV-12, GOV-03, §12 AI Governance Layer, §5 stage 11, §17 Phase 1).

## Objective
Minutes lifecycle: agenda-linked transcript, AI draft, secretary review, verification committee, approval and lock; AI Governance Layer with hard boundaries.

Phase boundary: Phase 1 implements manual minutes, editorial review, human verification, approval, and lock. AI transcription and AI-assisted drafting are Phase 2 and are not Phase 1 acceptance evidence.

## Minutes (GOV-12)
- Agenda-linked transcript
- AI draft
- Secretary review
- Verification committee
- Approval and lock
- Minutes cannot become final without configured human verification (Acceptance §19)

## AI Governance Layer (§12)
| Agent | Function | Hard boundary |
|---|---|---|
| AI AGM Planner | Timeline, readiness, checklist | Cannot mark legal compliance complete without evidence |
| AI Compliance Officer | Retrieve applicable rule and flag risk | Cannot issue final legal determination |
| AI Nomination Assistant | Document completeness and eligibility cues | Cannot approve/reject candidate |
| AI Chairman | Agenda/time/speaker procedural assistance | Cannot silence/remove participant autonomously |
| AI Secretary | Transcription and draft minutes | Cannot finalise minutes |
| AI Question Manager | Cluster duplicate questions | Cannot suppress governance record |

## Roles (§11)
- Minute Verification Committee: review and verify minutes.
- AGM Secretariat: create AGM, notices, documents, registration, minutes workflow.

## Acceptance criteria (this sprint)
- [ ] Minutes flow: AI draft → editorial review → verification → approved → locked.
- [ ] Human verification mandatory before minutes become final.
- [ ] AI AGM Planner cannot mark legal compliance complete without evidence.
- [ ] AI Compliance Officer cannot issue final legal determination.
- [ ] AI Nomination Assistant cannot approve/reject candidate.
- [ ] AI Secretary cannot finalise minutes.
- [ ] AI Question Manager cannot suppress governance record.
