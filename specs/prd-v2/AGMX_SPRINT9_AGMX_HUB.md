# AGMX v2.0 — Sprint 9 Spec: AGMX Hub

Source: AGMX Master PRD v2.0 (§7 Functional Requirements — AGMX Hub, §2 workspace table, §4 IA).

## Objective
Participant-facing workspace: single participant identity, My AGM dashboard, RSVP, AGM Pack viewer, motion tracking, nomination application, candidate gallery, voting centre, results/minutes access.

## Hub requirements (§7)
- Single participant identity and My AGM dashboard
- Notice acknowledgement and registration/RSVP
- AGM Pack with accessible document viewer
- Submit and track motions
- Nomination application / nomination workflow according to configured UUK
- Candidate Gallery with equal presentation template
- Join AGMX LIVE from one primary button on AGM day
- Personal voting centre showing only ballots for which participant is eligible
- Ballot receipt proving acceptance without revealing secret vote choice
- Results, approved minutes and previous resolution progress where authorised
- Senior/accessibility mode and low-bandwidth mode (WCAG, §15)

## IA mapping (§4)
AGMX Hub: Home / My AGM, Notis & Registration, AGM Pack

## Roles (§11)
- Member/Delegate: Hub access, participation and eligible voting.
- Candidate: own nomination/profile only.

## Acceptance criteria (this sprint)
- [ ] Participant has single identity and My AGM dashboard.
- [ ] Participant can acknowledge notice and RSVP.
- [ ] Participant can view AGM Pack with accessible document viewer.
- [ ] Participant can submit and track motions.
- [ ] Participant can apply for nomination per configured UUK workflow.
- [ ] Candidate gallery shows equal presentation template.
- [ ] One primary button joins AGMX LIVE on AGM day.
- [ ] Personal voting centre shows only eligible ballots.
- [ ] Results and approved minutes visible where authorised.
- [ ] Senior/accessibility and low-bandwidth modes available (WCAG).
